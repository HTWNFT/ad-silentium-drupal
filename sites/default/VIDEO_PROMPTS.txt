====================================================================
GLOBAL VIDEO LOOP STYLE (apply to EVERY PROMPT)
====================================================================
- 16:9 cinematic game environment video loop (6–8 seconds), seamless loop
- No readable text, no logos, no UI/HUD, no watermarks
- Cold rim light, steel/ash/obsidian palette, high-contrast silhouettes
- Controlled glow only (minimal, purposeful), militant thriller tone
- Motion: slow camera push or lateral drift + subtle parallax + atmospheric particles
- Keep scene readable at a distance (bold shapes, avoid micro-clutter)

GLOBAL AVOID (apply to every AVOID):
text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI/HUD, menus, captions, subtitles,
close-up faces, detailed characters, crowds, bright neon rainbow palettes,
excessive bloom, strobing lights, heavy lens flare, shakycam, muddy low contrast,
cartoon style, cute vibes, colorful coral/tropical vibes, gore, organs
