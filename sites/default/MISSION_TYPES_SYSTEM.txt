MISSION_TYPES_SYSTEM.txt

GOAL:
Define mission categories that:
1) Provide variety beyond combat (Ranger, hostage rescue, etc.)
2) Gate major power unlocks at milestone levels (Lv1 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 20)
3) Scale cleanly into Air / Ocean / Land routes until Boss Level (Warlords)

GLOBAL RULES (apply to ALL missions):
- Every mission has:
  - Primary Objective (required)
  - Secondary Objective (optional)
  - Fail State (clear)
  - Extraction Condition (how mission ends)
- Each mission awards:
  - XP (level progression)
  - Trial Credit(s) (used to unlock milestone powers)
  - Loot/Resources (craft/upgrade, if implemented)
- Mission difficulty tiers:
  - Tier 1: Lv1–4
  - Tier 2: Lv5–9
  - Tier 3: Lv10–14
  - Tier 4: Lv15–19
  - Tier 5: Lv20 Boss Level (Warlord)

TRIAL CREDITS (used for milestone power unlocks):
- [ ] Combat Trial Credit
- [ ] Ranger Trial Credit
- [ ] Hostage Trial Credit
- [ ] Containment Trial Credit
- [ ] Recovery Trial Credit

MILESTONE GATES (recommended):
- Lv1 unlock: No gate (starter kit online immediately)
- Lv5 unlock: Requires ANY 1 Trial Credit
- Lv10 unlock (Environment Mastery): Requires 1 Trial Credit + matching Route Credit
- Lv15 unlock (Anti-Warlord Tool): Requires ANY 2 Trial Credits (can be duplicates or mixed)
- Lv20 unlock (Crown Node / Boss Level): Requires 3 total Trial Credits + completion of a Tier 4 “Gatekeeper” mission

ROUTE CREDITS (environment mastery path):
- [ ] AIR Route Credit
- [ ] OCEAN Route Credit
- [ ] LAND Route Credit

ROUTE CREDIT RULE:
- A Route Credit is earned by completing a mission that has its core hazard in that environment
  (ex: AIR = vertical traversal + wind/shear hazards; OCEAN = pressure/oxygen; LAND = seismic/ruin traversal).

============================================================
MISSION TYPE 01 — COMBAT ASSAULT (DIRECT ENGAGEMENT)
Purpose:
- Baseline action loop, teaches enemies + player kit growth.

Primary Objectives (pick one):
- Eliminate target cell leader
- Clear an outpost / node
- Defeat roaming champion
- Break a blockade and reach extraction

Secondary Objectives (optional):
- Do not take downed state
- Keep allied NPC alive
- Finish under time

Fail States:
- Player wipe / timer fail / target escapes

Extraction:
- Reach evac zone OR hold area until signal confirms

Trial Credit:
- Grants: [Combat Trial Credit]
Route Credit (if themed):
- Optional based on map hazard: AIR / OCEAN / LAND

Variants:
- Breach & Clear (short, intense)
- Hunt (longer, multi-zone)
- Duel (single elite target)

============================================================
MISSION TYPE 02 — RANGER RECON (SCOUT / TRACK / SURVIVE)
Purpose:
- Your “not all combat” pillar. Stealth, tracking, survival, navigation, map mastery.

Primary Objectives:
- Scout and mark 3–5 points of interest
- Track a moving target and confirm ID
- Place signal beacons (no detection)
- Map a hazard corridor (air/ocean/land)

Secondary Objectives:
- Zero alarms triggered
- Recover bonus intel cache
- Complete without killing (optional pacifist run)

Fail States:
- Too many alarms / beacon destroyed / target lost

Extraction:
- Exfil by route, or upload recon data at uplink node

Trial Credit:
- Grants: [Ranger Trial Credit]
Route Credit:
- Often grants AIR / OCEAN / LAND if the recon hazard is dominant

Variants:
- Shadow Trail (tail a patrol)
- Dead Zone Survey (hazard-heavy)
- Signal Listening Post (timed uplink)

============================================================
MISSION TYPE 03 — HOSTAGE RESCUE (STEALTH + EXTRACT)
Purpose:
- High tension, route planning, crowd-control, non-lethal preference (optional).

Primary Objectives:
- Locate hostage(s)
- Free them (lock, cage, restraint)
- Escort to extraction

Secondary Objectives:
- No hostage damage
- No alarms (or max 1 alarm)
- Retrieve hostage’s carried item (key intel)

Fail States:
- Hostage death / hostage taken away / time expiration

Extraction:
- Escort to evac point OR defend evac while pickup arrives

Trial Credit:
- Grants: [Hostage Trial Credit]
Route Credit:
- If rescue occurs in dominant hazard zone (air rig, sea lab, land ruin), grant route credit.

Variants:
- Silent Lift (stealth favored)
- Running Evac (pursuit favored)
- Split Rescue (multiple targets)

============================================================
MISSION TYPE 04 — CONTAINMENT HOLD (DEFEND A ZONE)
Purpose:
- Teaches area denial, cooldown discipline, resource usage, defense builds.

Primary Objectives:
- Hold uplink for X waves
- Protect reactor / beacon / ritual device
- Keep a perimeter intact while data uploads

Secondary Objectives:
- Keep structure above 60% integrity
- Defeat optional elite wave
- Don’t leave zone

Fail States:
- Uplink destroyed / zone overrun / integrity hits zero

Extraction:
- After final wave, reach evac OR survive until timer ends

Trial Credit:
- Grants: [Containment Trial Credit]
Route Credit:
- If hazard is dominant (air platform, ocean pressure dome, land quake zone)

Variants:
- Wave Defense
- King-of-the-Hill (moving capture point)
- Escort-Hold (hold, then move, then hold)

============================================================
MISSION TYPE 05 — RECOVERY RETRIEVAL (SALVAGE / EXTRACT)
Purpose:
- Drives your economy (materials, artifacts, blueprints). Great for “theme production” loot tables later.

Primary Objectives:
- Retrieve 1–3 items (artifact, prototype, gene-tag relic, signal-cipher)
- Extract with item intact

Secondary Objectives:
- Retrieve bonus crate
- Don’t drop the payload
- Deliver to alternate extraction for extra reward

Fail States:
- Payload destroyed / lost / time out

Extraction:
- Reach extraction while carrying payload OR deposit into drop pod

Trial Credit:
- Grants: [Recovery Trial Credit]
Route Credit:
- Commonly grants route credit due to travel hazards

Variants:
- Heavy Carry (movement penalty)
- Timed Collapse (environment falling apart)
- Contested Recovery (rival scavengers)

============================================================
MISSION TYPE 06 — SABOTAGE INFILTRATION (NOISE VS SILENCE)
Purpose:
- Hybrid: stealth approach, explosive finish, tactical disengage.

Primary Objectives:
- Plant charges / overload system
- Disable comms tower / signal node
- Poison/patch a system (merged tech) or disrupt a discipline node (doomed)

Secondary Objectives:
- Leave without triggering lockdown
- Disable 2 extra subsystems
- Eliminate guard captain

Fail States:
- Lockdown completes / planted device disarmed

Extraction:
- Exfil immediately after sabotage OR survive pursuit

Trial Credit:
- Grants: [Ranger Trial Credit] OR [Combat Trial Credit] depending on approach
Route Credit:
- Optional

============================================================
MISSION TYPE 07 — ESCORT / CONVOY (PROTECT MOVING OBJECTIVE)
Purpose:
- Adds pacing variety; forces player to fight while repositioning.

Primary Objectives:
- Escort allied unit or payload to destination
- Keep convoy above X% health

Secondary Objectives:
- No stops / no repairs
- Save optional civilians / prisoners

Fail States:
- Payload destroyed / ally dies

Extraction:
- Arrive at safe zone

Trial Credit:
- Grants: [Containment Trial Credit] (defense) OR [Hostage Trial Credit] (if escorting persons)

============================================================
MISSION TYPE 08 — ELITE HUNT (GATEKEEPER / PRE-WARLORD)
Purpose:
- Tier 4 gate mission for Lv20 access (required by Milestone Gates).

Primary Objectives:
- Locate and eliminate Gatekeeper
- Survive unique mechanic phase (air/ocean/land themed)

Secondary Objectives:
- No downed state
- Clear optional elite entourage

Fail States:
- Player wipe / gatekeeper escapes

Extraction:
- Confirm kill + extract OR receive “boss-route clearance” signal

Trial Credit:
- Grants: [Combat Trial Credit] + one additional credit based on mission structure
Route Credit:
- Grants matching AIR/OCEAN/LAND Route Credit automatically

============================================================
BOSS LEVEL MISSION TYPE — WARLORD ASCENT (TIER 5)
Purpose:
- Final encounter: player reaches stat parity with Warlord at Lv20.
- Multi-phase fight with environment dominance.

Primary Objectives:
- Defeat Warlord (Air / Ocean / Land)
- Survive phase shift mechanic

Secondary Objectives:
- Optional: spare/claim/banish choice (if narrative supports)
- Optional: no damage phase

Fail States:
- Player wipe

Extraction:
- Auto-exit cutscene OR manual evac while world destabilizes

Trial Credit:
- Grants: Crown Node completion (endgame progression marker)
Route Credit:
- N/A (already completed)
