WARLORD_INDEX_AND_PROMPTS.txt
(Genetic Warlords / Elite Families — 9 Houses x 3 NPCs = 27 portraits)

GLOBAL STYLE (apply to every PROMPT):
- Square 1:1 game UI portrait on a fully transparent background (PNG with alpha)
- Centered shoulders-up (or tight chest-up) elite NPC portrait
- Skin tone: unified dark grey; eyes black; hair black (stringy/scraggly)
- Face: long, scraggly, generally ugly; physically unimposing (frail/slender)
- Wardrobe: ornate robes, jewels, opulent but sinister; no weapons focus
- Lighting: cold rim light, high-contrast silhouette, crisp edges, controlled glow only
- Collar ring + matte gene-tag plate with 3–5 geometric insets (NO readable text)
- EXACTLY ONE toxic accent dot (one dot only) per House (color below)
- No background/scene elements

HOUSE ACCENT DOT COLORS:
- Vesperin: magenta
- Calderoux: cyan
- Mournval: sickly green
- Dravessa: magenta
- Kyranth: cyan
- Aurelianth: sickly green
- Vahloran: cyan
- Sablehart: magenta
- Noxmere: sickly green

AVOID (paste with every generation):
AVOID: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, readable insignia, background scenery, full scenes, multiple accent colors, extra glow dots, neon bloom, lens flare, gore, exposed organs, messy splatter, attractive/pretty faces, heroic physiques, low contrast, clutter, crowds, weapons dominating the frame, thick borders, drop shadows outside the subject
