RONIN_PROMPT_RECIPE.txt
(45 Ronin Mutant Portraits — copy/paste ready for ChatGPT Images)

GLOBAL FORMAT RULES (apply to every prompt):
- Square 1:1 game UI portrait
- Fully transparent background (PNG with alpha)
- Centered waist-up (or tight chest-up if silhouette reads better)
- High-contrast silhouette, cold rim light, steel/ash/obsidian palette
- Ronin look: stripped/defaced House regalia, patched wraps, scavenged straps
- Collar ring is cracked/filed; gene-tag plate scratched/defaced
- Embed the “formal designation” via a blank gene-tag plate with 3–5 geometric insets (NO letters)
- EXACTLY ONE toxic accent dot (dim/flickering), matching origin House color family
- No background scene elements

AVOID (paste into every generation):
AVOID: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, readable insignia, background scenery, full scenes, multiple accent colors, extra glow dots, neon bloom, lens flare, gore, exposed organs, messy splatter, low contrast, clutter, crowds, weapons dominating the frame, thick borders, drop shadows outside the subject

COMBINE HUMAN AND ANIMAL TRAITS FACIALLY AMD PHYSICALLY 50%/50% ON ALL GENERATIONS FACIALLY AS WELL AS PHYSICALLY. Make the faces somehow similar to humans (mouths with modified teeth for speech) while remaining part animal. 

HOUSE ACCENT COLORS:
- Vesperin / Dravessa / Sablehart: magenta dot
- Calderoux / Kyranth / Vahloran: cyan dot
- Mournval / Aurelianth / Noxmere: sickly green dot

RONIN STATUS SLUR SET (for writing/barks; not to be drawn as text):
Nullbond; Oathless; Scrapcode; Driftblood; Misprint; Dead-Tag
