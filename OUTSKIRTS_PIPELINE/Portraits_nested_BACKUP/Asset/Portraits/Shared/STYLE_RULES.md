# Portrait Style Rules — OUTSKIRTS OF HELL

- Mood: doom-tier dread + far-future ruin + occult-radioactive undertone
- Lighting: high-contrast, readable silhouette; controlled glow (no blown highlights)
- Color: mostly steel/ash/obsidian; exactly one toxic accent (cyan OR magenta OR sickly green)
- Framing: head+shoulders; face readable; background kept simple; no readable text
